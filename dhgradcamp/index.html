What is Digital Humanities

Digital Humanites involves bringing digital tools and methods to bear on humanities questions, and a humanistic lens on the digital+technical.

Genres + Areas

Collecting and sharing source material

Digital editions, digital archives, bibliographies, 3D data

Narrative Experiences

Exhibitions, journal articles

Computational Interpretation

Text analysis, GIS + spatial analysis, data collection/curation

Working with the Scholars' Lab

Working with the Scholars' Lab

  • The Scholars' Lab approaches Digital Humanities both as an interdisciplinary field of study and a community of practice. In that sense, theory and practice share equal importance.
  • We prioritize teaching people how to do things on their own or in collaboration, instead of doing things for people.

Scholars' Lab's Role

  • Help you identify what areas and skills can most benefit your research interests;
  • Help you learn how to become better verses in those areas and skills to reach your research goals.

For Students

We also prioritize support for professional development through things like:

  • Portfolio development and review
  • Consults on conference proposals, articles, other academic work.
  • Mock presentations and interviews
