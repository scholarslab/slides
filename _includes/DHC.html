The heart of digital humanities at UVA

The Scholars' Lab is part of the Library's Digital Humanities Center. Established in 2023, the DHC brings together UVA’s eminent practitioners in digital humanities, the Institute for Advanced Technology in the Humanities (IATH) and the Scholars’ Lab, for a total of 16 full-time experts in a richly interdisciplinary repertoire. Complementing the specialist librarians and other research wings of the Library, we support members of the University and community, regardless of status, field, or subject area, to pursue their digital and experimental research and pedagogical projects.
