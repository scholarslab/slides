Scholars' Lab research co-conspirators: IATH

Since 1992, the Institute for Advanced Technology in the Humanities has collaborated with humanities researchers on a remarkable range of transformative, original research projects during selective fellowships. Faculty Resident and Associate Fellowship programs support UVA faculty doing humanities research, especially larger projects needing applications development and grant planning.
